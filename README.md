# Aircraft Galley & Lavatory Components: Cabin Service Parts for Aviation Maintenance

Aircraft galley and lavatory components support essential passenger and crew services throughout the cabin. Galleys can contain equipment for food and beverage preparation, storage, water service, waste handling, and electrical operation, while lavatories incorporate toilets, sinks, faucets, waste systems, lighting, ventilation, and supporting cabin hardware.

For airlines, MRO organizations, aircraft operators, cabin refurbishment companies, and aviation parts distributors, these systems create a broad range of maintenance and replacement requirements. Components may be replaced during scheduled maintenance, cabin refurbishment, equipment upgrades, troubleshooting, or repairs.

Because aircraft cabin configurations vary, procurement teams should identify the exact component, aircraft model, installation location, manufacturer part number, and system application whenever possible. This information helps suppliers locate the appropriate aviation replacement part.
